Design

Exclusivity expressed through considered design

The interior and décor were created by a leading Moscow design studio. Everything about the apartment is out of the ordinary: the materials, the design choices and the very feeling of stepping inside. Rich dark tones lend strength and depth, yet the space remains calm.

Every detail is put together to sustain an atmosphere of strength, comfort and ease — from the living room with its mural panel to the bedroom, where things are there not “for show” but as part of everyday life.

Living room

A concert hall and music salon

Dark tones here are not gloomy: wood, leather, display cabinets and chandelier light hold the room together. Next to it are a salon with a record collection, a grand piano and a space the presentation explicitly describes as a flexible-use room.

Kitchen

Kitchen and dining room

Soft diffused light, dark wood and display cabinets. An island for a quick breakfast and a separate table for long dinners.

Master bedroom

Bedroom and bathroom

Carved wood, burgundy textiles and pieces with a history. The bedroom has its own bathroom: a bathtub by the window, marble and a glass shower.

Second level

Bedroom and study

A separate room with an antique cabinet, a portrait and upholstered furniture — a second bedroom that works perfectly well as a study.

Private spa

Sauna, hammam and massage

A dedicated wellness suite: a massage table, a glass sauna and a hammam. Not a club on the ground floor of the building — these rooms are inside the apartment.

Double-height space

Winter garden, gym and terrace

A glass roof, plants and gym equipment in a single space. Next to it is a private terrace — outdoor access without a shared courtyard.

Location

Zarechye: between the park, Skolkovo and the city

The penthouse is in the Russkiy Avangard residential complex, Zarechye, Odintsovsky district, Moscow region. Travel times below are as stated in the property presentation.

  1. 1

    Meshchersky Park10 minutes on foot

    A forest that has never been cleared. In the 16th century Ivan the Terrible hunted here; later it belonged to the Trubetskoy, Dolgorukov and Tretyakov families. Today it has more than 19 km of running and cycling paths and workout areas, including ones for children.

  2. 2

    Skolkovo15 minutes by car

    The innovation centre is close by: exhibitions, master classes, seminars on contemporary art and events that bring tech teams together.

    Even with heavy traffic, the business district is about half an hour away by car.

  4. 4

    Government buildings and Teatralnaya Square are about 40 minutes away by car.
